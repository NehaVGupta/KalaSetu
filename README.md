
# KalaSetu – Your Art. Your Terms.

**Empowering Local Artists Through Smart Bookings**

KalaSetu is an AI-assisted platform that connects folk artists, craft demonstrators, local guides, and heritage hosts with event organizers and tourists.

It addresses challenges such as limited visibility, unclear pricing, uncertain bookings, irregular income, and delayed payments through a structured and transparent booking process.

## Key Features

- **Artist Registration:** Create profiles showcasing art forms, skills, location, and portfolio.
- **Smart Artist Matching:** Find suitable artists based on event requirements, location, budget, and specialization.
- **Wishlist:** Save preferred artist groups for future bookings.
- **Booking Requests:** Organizers can share event details and send booking requests.
- **Artist-Controlled Negotiation:** Artists can accept, counteroffer, or decline requests.
- **Booking Management:** View confirmed bookings, agreed terms, and booking history.
- **Payment Tracking:** Record payments and monitor outstanding balances.

## Website Workflow

1. **Explore:** Enter the platform as an Artist, Organizer, or Tourist.
2. **Register:** Artists create and publish their profiles with relevant details and skills.
3. **Discover & Match:** Organizers search for artists and receive suggestions based on event requirements.
4. **Request & Negotiate:** Organizers send booking requests, while artists can accept, counteroffer, or decline.
5. **Confirm:** Both parties agree on booking details, performance fees, and requirements.
6. **Track Payments:** Organizers record payments and monitor outstanding balances until completion.

## Technology & Prototype Notes

- Browser-based interactive prototype.
- Deterministic demo rules for artist matching.
- Browser LocalStorage for saving demo data.
- Fictional artist profiles and simulated payment records.
- No real authentication, online transactions, or backend integration.

## How to Run

1. Download or clone the repository.
2. Extract the project folder.
3. Open `welcome.html` in any modern web browser.
4. Explore the KalaSetu platform.
